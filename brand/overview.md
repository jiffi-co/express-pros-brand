# Overview

Express Employment Professionals is a staffing and recruitment business that runs as a franchise network. Every office is owned by someone who lives in the market it serves, and every office is backed by an international head office that has been doing this since 1983. That combination is the whole proposition, and Express states it plainly: locally owned, global resources.

In Australia and New Zealand the group operates as Express Employment Professionals ANZ, with its corporate office at Level 5, 50 York Street, Sydney. It is a growing network rather than a settled one, which is why franchise recruitment sits alongside staffing as a first-class audience.

## The one thing not to get wrong

Express places people, and it never charges those people a fee. That is not a promotion, it is a standing commitment, and it appears on almost every job seeker page they publish. Any Express material that implies a candidate pays for anything is wrong before it is even off-brand.

## Positioning in one sentence

Express connects people and jobs through locally owned offices that carry the process, the scale, and the four decades of hiring experience of a global staffing leader.

## Vision, purpose, tagline

- **Vision.** To help as many people as possible find good jobs by helping as many clients as possible find good people.
- **Purpose.** To help people succeed.
- **Mission.** To professionally market and provide quality human resources solutions through an international franchising network that benefits our associates, customers, franchisees, corporate staff, stockholders, and communities in which we conduct business.
- **Tagline.** Respecting People. Impacting Business.&trade;

The tagline is two halves of one promise. *Respecting People* is the compassion side, how Express treats candidates, associates, and clients. *Impacting Business* is the commercial side, the sharp business sense that makes the compassion sustainable. Express calls the pair its Care Promise. Use both halves or neither.

## Personality

Warm, direct, credible, practical, local. Express is not a disruptor and does not want to sound like one. It sounds like an experienced recruiter who has placed a lot of people, knows the businesses in town, and will tell you straight what is achievable.

## What Express calls things

The vocabulary is specific and worth matching exactly.

| Express says | Not |
| --- | --- |
| Associate (someone on Express payroll, placed with a client) | Temp, worker, resource |
| Client company | Customer, account |
| Job seeker | Candidate pool, talent pipeline |
| Franchise Owner | Franchisee (acceptable, but Owner is the warmer form Express prefers in marketing) |
| Office | Branch, location (use "office" for the business, "location" only on the finder) |
| Workforce solutions | Staffing products |

## The brand family

Four brands sit under Express Employment International. Do not mix their logos or their colours.

| Brand | What it is | Mark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Express Employment Professionals | The flagship staffing brand. Everything in this repo is for this one unless stated otherwise. | Three blues |
| Express Employment International | The parent brand for the whole network. Used in corporate and international contexts. | Three blues, same lock-up, different descriptor |
| Specialized Recruiting Group | Professional and executive search | Teal, its own spiral mark |
| Express Healthcare Staffing | Healthcare staffing | Express lock-up with a red heartbeat rule |

Only the Express Employment Professionals artwork ships here. If a piece of work needs Specialized Recruiting Group or Express Healthcare Staffing, pull the official files from [ExpressPros.com/Logos](https://www.expresspros.com/newsroom/logos) rather than recolouring anything in this repo. Note that *Specialized* keeps its American spelling in every market, because it is a proper noun.
