## Notes for anyone writing bios or an about page

- **Adam Hislop-Reynolds**, Group CEO, brings more than 25 years in recruitment and staffing, including senior roles at Adecco, Toll People, The Skilled Group and Healthcare Australia, where he helped scale the business to a billion dollars inside a decade. The angle Express leads with is people-first leadership, not the balance sheet.
- **Michelle Gamble**, CMO, ran her own marketing agency for 20 years and has worked across several franchise groups. Her own bio is written in first person, which is worth matching if you are extending the page.
- **Kim D. Griffin** is the link back to Express Employment International. She started as a Sales Representative in the Oklahoma City office in 1995 and has coached more than 800 franchisees. Useful whenever a piece needs to show that the ANZ business is not a licensee at arm's length.
- **Regional leadership** is expanding. Janine Bradley is Regional Manager for South East Queensland and Matt leads Auckland. Both have interviews on the ANZ blog, and both are the right voice for a market-entry story.

## House style for bios

The live page mixes third person and first person, because some people wrote their own. That is fine and it reads human. If you are adding one, follow the person's existing voice rather than normalising the page.

Bios run roughly 80 to 150 words and follow a consistent shape: years of experience, where that experience came from, then what the person actually cares about. Several close on something personal, which is on brand for a company whose tagline starts with *Respecting People*. Keep those.

Every bio on the live page ends with an "Email [first name]" link. Preserve that pattern if you rebuild the page.
