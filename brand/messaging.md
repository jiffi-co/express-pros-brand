# Messaging

Express has three audiences and speaks to each one differently. Get the audience right first, and most of the messaging decisions follow.

| Audience | What they want to know | Where they land |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job seekers | Will you find me work, and what does it cost me | expresspros.com.au job seeker pages |
| Employers | Can you send me someone good, and how fast | expresspros.com.au employer pages |
| Prospective Franchise Owners | Can I build a business out of this, and what does it take | expressfranchising.com.au |

## Elevator pitch

**One line.** Express Employment Professionals connects people and jobs through locally owned offices, backed by four decades of global staffing experience.

**Three lines.** Express Employment Professionals is a staffing and recruitment network with more than 860 locally owned offices worldwide, including Australia and New Zealand. Every office is owned by someone in the community it serves, and every associate is screened through an ISO-registered selection process before they start. Job seekers never pay a fee.

**A paragraph.** Express Employment Professionals has been putting people to work since 1983. Today the network places more than half a million people a year through offices that are locally owned and internationally supported. In Australia and New Zealand, Express recruits across Office Services, Light Industrial, Skilled Trades and Professional roles, on temporary, contract and permanent terms. Employers get a local team that knows their market and a candidate process that is ISO-registered end to end. Job seekers get access to multiple openings from a single application, and never pay a fee. The promise on both sides is the same one Express has carried for four decades: Respecting People. Impacting Business.

## Headline formulas

### Job seekers

The shape is *a plain instruction, or a plain reassurance*. Never a question, because the reader already knows they need a job.

- **Verb + object.** "Find a job." "Search job openings." "Start your job journey."
- **Objection, answered.** "The job search doesn't need to be lonely." "You will never pay a fee."
- **One connection, many outcomes.** "One connection with Express gives you access to multiple job opportunities."

### Employers

The shape is *a capability, stated as a fact*.

- **Claim + proof.** "Locally owned, global resources." "Any position, any industry."
- **Need + timing.** "The workforce solutions you need, when you need them."
- **Contrast.** "There's a big difference between the right skills, and the right fit."

### Franchise

The shape is *a challenge, then the reassurance*. This is the only register where Express asks the reader a question.

- **Challenge.** "Tired of building someone else's business?" "Ready to back yourself, without going it alone?"
- **Invitation with the risk removed.** "Start your labour hire business without losing your salary." "Build a business helping people find jobs. Change lives, including your own."
- **Ranked claim.** "Invest in a top recruitment and staffing franchise." Always attribute the ranking.

## Calls to action

Every Express CTA is an imperative verb, and almost every page ends by pointing at a local office. Use these exactly.

| Context | Copy |
| --- | --- |
| Job seeker, primary | Search job openings |
| Job seeker, secondary | Find a location |
| Employer, primary | Explore workforce solutions |
| Employer, secondary | Contact a local office today! |
| Either | Find your local office |
| Newsletter, job seeker | Sign up for our Job Journey Newsletter |
| Newsletter, employer | Sign up for our Refresh Leadership Newsletter |
| Franchise, primary | Get started |
| Franchise, secondary | Learn more |
| Recruit To Own | Let's talk |

Never "Click here", "Submit", "Learn more about our services", or "Get in touch".

## Newsletters

Two named newsletters, two audiences. Do not cross the streams.

- **Job Journey.** Job seekers. Career development, interview preparation, the job search.
- **Refresh Leadership.** Employers and managers. Hiring, retention, team leadership.

## Structure of a page

The order Express uses, and a safe default:

1. Audience-specific hero with one action.
2. The positioning paragraph. Locally owned, globally backed.
3. The three service lines, or the three steps of the process.
4. A credentialed statistic on a gradient.
5. One closing ask, pointing at a local office.

One primary action per screen. If a page is asking two things, it is two pages.
