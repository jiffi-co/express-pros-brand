# Services

Express sells two things to two audiences: workforce solutions to employers, and access to work to job seekers. Underneath both sits one recruitment process. The franchise offer is a third product entirely, sold to a third audience.

Service line names are capitalised because they are Express's names for them, not descriptions.

## Service lines

### Office Services

Clerical and administrative, receptionist, customer service representative, call centre, office manager, data entry, executive assistant, and similar.

Positioning: the roles that keep a business running day to day, filled by people who can start without a long ramp-up.

### Light Industrial

Assembler, forklift driver, welder, lathe operator, maintenance worker, shipping and receiving clerk, warehouse worker, machinist, and similar.

Positioning: volume and reliability. This is where Express's ability to move quickly matters most, because a line that is short two people today is short two people today.

### Skilled Trades

CNC machinist, forklift driver, truck driver, HVAC technician, facilities maintenance, and similar.

Positioning: qualified, ticketed people whose skills are verified before they arrive.

### Professional

Higher-level and specialist roles, permanent or contract. Sits alongside Specialized Recruiting Group, the sister brand built specifically for professional and executive search.

Positioning: the search is slower on purpose, because the cost of the wrong senior hire is the whole point.

Note that the ANZ site is inconsistent about whether the third line is called Skilled Trades or Professional, and lists all four across different pages. Check the page you are writing for rather than assuming.

## Engagement types

| Type | What it is | When an employer reaches for it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temporary and contract staffing | Assignments from a few days to several months, associate on the Express payroll | Fluctuating demand, seasonal peaks, cover, project work |
| Evaluation hire | Evaluate an associate on the job in your own workplace before committing to a permanent offer | When experience and qualifications look right but cultural fit is unproven |
| Direct hire | Permanent placement | A role that needs to be filled once, properly |
| Professional and executive search | Recruiters working a higher-level brief, career or contract | Senior, specialist, or hard-to-source roles |

Evaluation hire has its own established argument, and it is one of the sharper pieces of Express copy: there is a big difference between the right skills and the right fit; employee turnover is costly, so when a new hire does not work out, the expenses of replacing them add up.

## The candidate recruitment process

The process is the product. It is ISO-registered, and Express says so on almost every employer page. When describing it, keep the two facts that matter to each side:

- **To an employer.** Every associate is screened and evaluated for skills, abilities, and cultural fit before they are placed. That is a documented, audited process, not a phone screen.
- **To a job seeker.** One application, matched against openings at multiple client companies, with help preparing for the interview. Two ways to apply: call the local office and complete it over the phone, or apply at ExpressPros.com.

Express uses both "ISO-certified" and "ISO-registered" across its pages. Prefer **ISO-registered**, which is the wording in the current employer copy, and stay consistent within a single piece.

## What Express does not charge for

Job seekers never pay a fee. This appears on the job seeker landing page, the types-of-jobs page, and the workforce solutions page, which tells you how load bearing it is. Any material that leaves it ambiguous is wrong.

## The franchise offer

A separate product, on a separate site, in the franchise register. See [visual-system.md](./visual-system.md) for the register switch.

### Franchise ownership

The core offer for people buying into the network. What Express leads with:

- Fully funded payroll. Express Corporate processes payroll, so cash flow is not the owner's problem.
- On-the-ground support from the Sydney head office plus the Franchise Owner network.
- #1 staffing and recruiting franchise, ranked by Entrepreneur.
- Average Franchise Owner tenure of 12 years.
- A corporate team focused entirely on Franchise Owner support, training, and growth.

### Recruit To Own

An ANZ-specific pathway, and the most distinctive thing in the ANZ offer. It is aimed at experienced recruiters who want to own a business without giving up an income while they build it.

Published terms, as at August 2026. Quote them exactly or not at all.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Recruitment model | Temporary labour hire |
| Salary | $100K plus $10K car allowance plus super |
| Employment term | Up to 18 months |
| Franchise fee discount after the term | 50% |
| Incentive structure | Yes |
| Finance | Bridge to Ownership Loan available |

The development program covers business acumen and metrics, self-leadership through goal setting, team management, and a path to ownership.
